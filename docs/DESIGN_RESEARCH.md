## Two internal concepts

**A. Field notebook / atmosphere.** Cool porcelain, ink, mineral blue; Newsreader paired with Manrope; a dark generative contour plate against open editorial space. Research stories behave like reading a notebook with evidence pinned beside the argument.
**B. Screening room.** Dark full-screen project chapters, large stills, compact navigation, curtains between pages. Strong cinema, but sustained darkness and scene changes impede technical scanning and require photography we do not possess.

Selected A: better balance of the user's 45% editorial / 40% cinematic / 15% scientific intent. Retain B's single dark opening plate, not an entire theatrical interface.

## Original opportunity and critique before code

The connective subject is observation: atmospheric observations, computational evaluation, photographic attention. Use an original procedural flow engraving as a visual metaphor, explicitly labelled illustrative. Make limitations part of each research story rather than hiding them in fine print. Avoid a giant generic slogan, identical project cards, numbered decoration, pill tags, pure cream/terracotta, copied imagery and invented photographs. A mixed serif/sans identity with an off-centre split opening is specific to this notebook/exhibition brief. One signature motif carries the boldness; the rest reads calmly.
