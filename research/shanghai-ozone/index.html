SURF-2026-0008 / Undergraduate research

Shanghai Ozone ML

Can a weather catalogue fitted on ozone-pollution days explain the wider year—and can a model predict a year it has never seen?

Source repository ↗

Context

Shanghai
2021–2025
SOM · Random Forest · SHAP

A catalogue, with boundaries.

The study combines ERA5 synoptic fields, repeated Self-Organizing Maps, frozen-catalogue projection and leave-one-year-out Random Forest models. The matched analysis spans 1,826 Beijing-local days, including 142 verified pollution days with MDA8 O₃ > 160 μg m⁻³.

K6 had higher mean label-invariant stability than K4 (ARI 0.636 versus 0.573), but remained a provisional, medium-confidence catalogue. Only 43.0% of all matched-day projections fell within the pollution-training 95th-percentile distance threshold; in winter, the share was 0.9%.

Prediction outside the fitting year.

Five leave-one-year-out folds hold each year out of predictor-model fitting. The public model comparison reports the following out-of-fold results.

Methods and research role

Four standardised ERA5 field blocks feed PCA; PCA95 retains 36 components. K4 and K6 formal families each use 30 fixed seeds. Representative runs use the mean-ARI medoid rather than the most visually convenient run.

After selection, PCA and SOM prototypes are frozen for projection. SHAP values are calculated for held-out predictions, using fold-specific training backgrounds.

Han Che was the formally appointed undergraduate researcher and led the core data-science workflow. Hongxuan Li and Xinrui Li were SURF volunteers. Dr. Lei Han supervised the work through XJTLU and the Shanghai Academy of Environmental Sciences, as documented in the repository.

Association is not causation.

SHAP describes predictive attribution. Atmospheric radicals were not directly measured, and this work does not estimate radical concentrations or establish causal effects. Cool-season catalogue applicability is limited. Raw partner observations are restricted; a full upstream rerun requires authorised inputs.

The public repository includes research code, selected outputs and a poster preview. No peer-reviewed publication is claimed here. Original figures and code are not republished in this portfolio because ownership and redistribution permission remain unconfirmed.
